SnAr Reaction Optimization

Optimization Problem

\[ \begin{aligned} \min_{t_{res},\,\text{eq}_{morph},\,C_{DFNB},\,T}\quad & \left\{\, -\,\text{STY},\;\; E_{factor} \,\right\} \\[4pt] \text{s.t.}\quad & 0.5\ \text{min} < t_{res} < 2\ \text{min} \\ & 1\ \text{eq} < \text{Morpholine} < 5\ \text{eq} \\ & 0.1\ \text{M} < C_{DFNB} < 0.5\ \text{M} \\ & 60\ ^\circ\text{C} < T < 140\ ^\circ\text{C} \end{aligned} \] \[ E_{factor} = \frac{m_{\text{undesired products}}}{m_{\text{o-FNM}}} \]

Variables

SymbolDescription
\(t_{res}\)Residence time (decision)
MorpholineMorpholine equivalents (decision)
\(C_{DFNB}\)DFNB concentration (decision)
\(T\)Reaction temperature (decision)
STYSpace-time yield

Source problem

Schweidtmann, A.M., Clayton, A.D., Holmes, N., Bradford, E., Bourne, R.A., Lapkin, A.A. (2018). Machine learning meets continuous flow chemistry: Automated optimization towards the Pareto front of multiple objectives. Chemical Engineering Journal, 352, 277–282. doi:10.1016/J.CEJ.2018.07.031
