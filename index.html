NEMOPSO

A Graph Theory-Based
Algorithm for Chemical Engineering Optimization Problems

In this work, we developed NEMOPSO a graph-based multi-objective particle swarm optimization algorithm, to efficiently solve complex engineering optimization problems involving competing objectives.

The method combines the exploration capabilities of particle swarm optimization with graph-theoretic representations of the solution population, enabling improved leader selection, diversity preservation, and convergence toward the Pareto-optimal region.

We evaluate NEMOPSO on a comprehensive set of multi-objective benchmark problems, including the ZDT and DTLZ test suites, covering problems with different Pareto-front geometries, dimensions, and levels of difficulty. The developed framework is designed for direct application to chemical engineering optimization problems, where multiple process objectives must often be optimized simultaneously.

By combining multi-objective optimization, graph theory, and process simulation, NEMOPSO provides a flexible computational framework for identifying trade-offs in complex chemical engineering systems. The resulting Pareto-optimal solutions can help engineers understand the relationship between competing design objectives and select process configurations that provide an effective balance between economic performance, energy consumption, and process efficiency.
