TRM Reactor Optimization

Optimization Problem

\[ \begin{aligned} \min_{T_{in},\,F_{O_2}/F_{CH_4},\,F_{CO_2}/F_{CH_4}}\quad & \left\{\, -\,H_2/CO,\;\; -\,\eta_{reactor} \,\right\} \\[4pt] \text{s.t.}\quad & 600\ ^\circ\text{C} < T_{in} < 1000\ ^\circ\text{C} \\ & 0.3 < F_{O_2}/F_{CH_4} < 0.6 \\ & 1 < F_{CO_2}/F_{CH_4} < 3 \\ & \text{Momentum, mass and heat transfer (COMSOL)} \end{aligned} \] \[ \eta_{reactor} = \frac{\dot m_{H_2}\,\mathrm{LHV}_{H_2} + \dot m_{CO}\,\mathrm{LHV}_{CO}}{\dot m_{CH_4,in}\,\mathrm{LHV}_{CH_4}} \]

Variables

SymbolDescription
\(T_{in}\)Inlet temperature (decision)
\(F_{O_2}/F_{CH_4}\)O2/CH4 feed ratio (decision)
\(F_{CO_2}/F_{CH_4}\)CO2/CH4 feed ratio (decision)
\(\mathrm{LHV},\ \dot m\)Lower heating value and mass flux

Source problem

Osat, M., Shojaati, F., Hafizi, A. (2023). A multi-objective optimization of three conflicting criteria in a methane tri-reforming reactor. International Journal of Hydrogen Energy, 48, 6275–6287. doi:10.1016/J.IJHYDENE.2022.06.221
